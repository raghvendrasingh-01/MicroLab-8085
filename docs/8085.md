# The 8085 in MicroLab

## Coverage

All 251 valid opcode encodings of the 8085 are implemented — the full
instruction set, including the 8085-specific `RIM` and `SIM`. The five
opcodes that are invalid on the 8085 (`CB`, `D9`, `DD`, `ED`, `FD`) are
rejected: executing one halts with an error, and disassembling one reports
`DB xxH ; invalid opcode`. The alternate NOP slots (`08`, `10`, `18`,
`28`, `38`) decode as NOP, like the real chip.

| Group | Instructions |
|---|---|
| Data transfer | MOV, MVI, LDA, STA, LHLD, SHLD, LDAX, STAX, XCHG |
| Arithmetic | ADD, ADI, ADC, ACI, SUB, SUI, SBB, SBI, INR, DCR, INX, DCX, DAD |
| Logical | ANA, ANI, ORA, ORI, XRA, XRI, CMA, CMC, STC, CMP, CPI, RLC, RRC, RAL, RAR |
| Branch | JMP + 8 conditions, CALL + 8 conditions, RET + 8 conditions, RST 0-7, PCHL |
| Stack | PUSH, POP, XTHL, SPHL |
| I/O | IN port, OUT port |
| Control | NOP, HLT, EI, DI, RIM, SIM |

## Flags

S, Z, AC, P, CY are implemented per the Intel spec, including the
documented quirks: AC is computed from the nibble carry in all add/subtract
forms; P is parity of the result (1 = even); INX/DCX affect *no* flags;
DAD sets only CY; CMC/STC touch only CY. The flag panel shows all five
plus the pair they come from after every step.

## Timing

Every opcode carries its T-state count (with the taken/not-taken branch
split), accumulated into the machine's T-state total — the number the
oscilloscope traces against and the status bar shows. At the default 2 MHz
assumption, 20,000 T-states ≈ 10 ms.

## The debugger

- **Run / Pause / Step / Reset** from the toolbar or keyboard.
- **Breakpoints**: click the address gutter of a listing line (the store
  tracks them in the machine; hitting one pauses).
- **Current instruction**: the disassembly view highlights the instruction
  at PC and the memory panel highlights the bytes under it.
- **Speed**: Slow (1 instruction per frame — watch a keypad scan), Medium,
  Fast, or Max (time-budgeted, hundreds of thousands per frame).
- Registers A/B/C/D/E/H/L, SP, PC and the flags update after every step.

## Assembler

Two passes: labels and `EQU` on the first, encoding on the second.
Supported: `ORG`, `DB` (bytes or quoted strings), `DW`, `EQU`, labels,
hex (`80H`, `0FFH`), decimal, binary (`1010B`), quoted characters (`'A'`),
and `;` comments. Errors carry line numbers and a specific message, e.g.
`Line 4: Invalid operand for OUT — expected an 8-bit I/O address.`
The editor underlines the failing lines and the console lists all of them.
